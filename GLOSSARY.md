# Glossary

## Core Concepts

- **Companion**: The central engine that evaluates task states to provide dynamic, contextual greetings and prompts.
- **DailyTask**: A daily recurring habit, independent of a scheduled date. Its completion history records days on which it remains completed; undoing today's completion leaves earlier days intact.
- **TodoItem**: A single, non-recurring task associated with one calendar date.
- **Note**: A single, unstructured block of text used for transient thoughts.
- **DailyNote**: A note tied to one specific local calendar date.
